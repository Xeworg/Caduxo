# Research — caduxo-lot-movement-ledger

## Scope

Formal research for common inventory movement vocabulary and audit-log practices that can inform Caduxo's lot movement ledger. Caduxo remains a local-first product expiry tracker, not POS, billing, payments, accounting, or a full inventory management system.

Retrieval time: 2026-09-15.

## Evidence excerpts

### E1 — Reason-coded inventory adjustments

Source: S1.

> "Adjustment reasons let applications record why an inventory quantity changed, using the same set of reasons that sellers see in the Square Dashboard and Square Point of Sale."

> "With adjustment reasons, your application can: Write adjustments with a reason, so they're categorized consistently... Filter the inventory change history by one or more reasons."

> "Standard reasons are predefined by Square... Each standard reason implies a direction: `RECEIVED` `INCREASE`, `RETURNED` `INCREASE`, `DAMAGED` `DECREASE`, `THEFT` `DECREASE`, `LOST` `DECREASE`, `SPOILAGE_WASTE` `DECREASE`, `SAMPLES_PROMOTIONAL` `DECREASE`, `INTERNAL_USE` `DECREASE`, `VENDOR_RETURN` `DECREASE`, `PRODUCTION_WASTE` `DECREASE`."

> "Some reasons are only ever attached to adjustments generated by Square products... `SALE`, `RECOUNT`, `TRANSFER`, `IN_TRANSIT`, and `CANCELED_SALE`."

Interpretation for Caduxo: reason-specific stock changes are common. Caduxo can use a fixed V1 vocabulary with sale as a non-financial stock-out reason, plus waste/merma, expired/spoilage, damaged, internal use, supplier return, count/adjustment, transfer, and other.

### E2 — Ledger as source of truth and audit trail

Source: S2.

> "Every change to your on-hand stock — a receipt, a sale, an adjustment, a transfer, a stock-take reconciliation, a manufacturing run — is recorded as a row in the inventory movement ledger. The ledger is the single source of truth for how a product's quantity and value reached their current state."

> "A movement is one atomic change to inventory... Each row records: What moved — the product and a signed quantity. Where — the warehouse, and... bin location. What kind of change — a type such as Inbound Receipt, Sale, Adjustment, Transfer, Stock Take, Return, Purchase Return... What caused it — a link to the source document... plus a cached human-readable reference."

> "Together these are what make the ledger a true audit trail: given any quantity or valuation figure... you can trace it back to the exact movements that produced it."

Interpretation for Caduxo: a canonical movement ledger fits the user's need to answer "what happened to this lot?" and supports deriving current location balances from signed movement history. Caduxo should omit monetary/cost fields because they conflict with product non-goals.

### E3 — Immutable history and corrections by compensating events

Source: S2.

> "The ledger is immutable and system-generated. You never create, edit, or delete a movement by hand... the ledger UI is purely read, search, filter, drill-down, and export."

> "Because the ledger is generated rather than entered, you correct inventory by making a new offsetting movement (an adjustment, a stock take, a reversal in the source document) — never by editing history."

Interpretation for Caduxo: Caduxo should avoid editing/deleting prior movement rows. User actions such as move, salida, discard, and adjustment emit movements. Corrections are compensating movements, not historical edits.

### E4 — Transfer as paired source/destination transactions

Source: S2.

> "Move stock between bins" is listed as a subsystem that emits ledger movements.

Source: S3.

> "Use transfer journals to transfer items between stocking locations, batches, or product variants without associating any cost implications."

> "When you use a transfer journal, you must specify both the 'from' and 'to' inventory dimensions... The on-hand inventory for the defined inventory dimensions changes accordingly."

> "When you post a transfer journal, two inventory transactions are created for each journal line: An inventory issue at the 'from' location. An inventory receipt at the 'to' location."

Interpretation for Caduxo: moving 20 units from bodega to exhibición should be modeled as one user-visible transfer that decreases source balance and increases destination balance while preserving the lot total.

### E5 — Movement, adjustment, counting vocabulary

Source: S3.

> "Use inventory journals to post physical inventory transactions of various types, such as the posting of issues and receipts, inventory movements... and the reconciliation of physical inventory."

> "The following types of inventory journals are available: Movement, Inventory adjustment, Transfer, BOM, Item arrival, Production input, Counting, Tag counting."

> "Counting journals let you correct the current on-hand inventory... and then post the actual physical count, so that you can make the adjustments that are required to reconcile the differences."

Interpretation for Caduxo: the distinction between transfer/movement, adjustment, and count reconciliation is a common product pattern. Caduxo can expose a simple `Ajustar conteo` action for differences between physical count and system balance.

### E6 — Inventory history dimensions (low-weight corroboration)

Source: S4 snippets (direct Help Center fetch blocked for one page; Shopify developer schema surfaced through source-check snippets).

> Shopify inventory adjustment history / developer schema snippets mention dimensions including adjustment date, staff member, reason, inventory state, app, SKU, and source document.

Interpretation for Caduxo: optional movement metadata should include occurred_at, actor/source when available, reason, SKU/product/lot, location, and reference/note. Because Caduxo appears single-user today, actor can be optional or system-local until auth exists.

## Claims and implications

### C1 — Caduxo should use reason-coded stock events

Supported by: E1, E5.

Implication: V1 reason vocabulary should be fixed and small, not a free-text-only field. Recommended V1:

- `sale` — stock-out only; no price, payment, customer, tax, invoice, or accounting fields.
- `waste` / `merma`.
- `expired`.
- `damaged`.
- `internal_consumption`.
- `return_to_supplier` / supplier change.
- `inventory_adjustment` / count correction.
- `other` with required note.

### C2 — Caduxo should model current balances as derived from movement history

Supported by: E2, E3.

Implication: The ledger should be canonical. If performance needs it, current balances may be cached/materialized, but the user-facing truth remains the movement history.

### C3 — Caduxo should not edit prior movement history

Supported by: E3.

Implication: Corrections emit compensating movements. UI should avoid an "edit movement" path except possibly limited metadata notes in a later version; V1 should be append-only.

### C4 — Caduxo should represent internal transfers as one user action with balanced source/destination effects

Supported by: E4.

Implication: Moving 20 units from bodega to exhibición decreases bodega by 20 and increases exhibición by 20. The lot total remains unchanged.

### C5 — Caduxo should keep `sale` explicitly non-financial

Supported by product non-goals, E1 as vocabulary evidence only.

Implication: `sale` is a stock-out reason. Do not add price, total, payment method, customer, invoice, tax, margin, or POS flows.

## Proposal-ready decisions already confirmed by user

- Canonical ledger is source of truth.
- Existing partial resolutions should migrate into ledger.
- V1 reason vocabulary should be complete, not minimal.
- Expired stock exit is manual: Caduxo alerts/surfaces expiration, but user confirms physical discard and location.
- Batch auto-generation is readable/date-based and only when the batch field is blank; manual batch is respected.
- Lot creation emits an initial movement event (`Entrada inicial`) for the full quantity into the chosen initial location.

## Remaining proposal questions

1. Exact auto-batch format: e.g. `LOT-YYYYMMDD-001`, `{SKU}-YYYYMMDD-001`, or product-prefix fallback.
2. Whether V1 requires an initial location for every new lot, or allows a store-level "unassigned" entry position.
3. Whether `other` requires a note. Recommendation: yes.
4. Whether actor/source is stored now as `system`/local user placeholder, or postponed until auth exists.
5. How much historical `lot_resolution_events` data must be surfaced in the UI after migration.
